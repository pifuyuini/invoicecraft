隐私政策

最后更新于 2026 年 9 月 22 日

我们收集的信息

账户数据包括您的邮箱、姓名和使用 Argon2 处理的密码摘要，不存储明文密码。业务数据包括客户、开票方信息、账单、明细和偏好。支付由 Stripe 处理，我们仅保存 Stripe 客户标识与订阅状态，不接触或存储银行卡号。我们使用签名会话 Cookie 保持登录，不使用广告或追踪 Cookie。

信息用途

信息仅用于提供服务，包括账户认证、保存账单和处理订阅。我们不会出售您的数据，也不将其用于广告。

分享

任何持有公开链接的人都可以查看对应账单，请谨慎分享。需要处理付款时，我们会与 Stripe 共享必要数据；法律要求时，我们也可能披露数据。

保留与删除

账户有效期间，您的数据会被保留。申请删除账户和全部数据，请通过网站公布的联系方式联系我们；我们将在 30 天内删除您的数据。

安全

密码使用 Argon2 处理，会话使用签名 Cookie。正式部署应使用 HTTPS。任何传输方式都不能保证绝对安全，建议使用独立密码。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

What we collect

  • Account data — your email address, name, and a one-way (Argon2) hash of your password. We never store plaintext passwords.
  • Business data — the clients, invoices, and line items you create.
  • Payment data — handled entirely by Stripe; we never see or store your card number. We store only your Stripe customer ID and subscription status.
  • Technical data — a signed session cookie to keep you logged in. No advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

Solely to operate the Service: authenticating you, storing your invoices, and processing your subscription. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Sharing

Invoice "public links" are visible to anyone you share them with — share them only with your clients. We share data with Stripe as needed to process payments, and we may disclose data if required by law.

Retention & deletion

Your data is kept while your account is active. To request full account and data deletion, contact us at the email address published on our website; we will delete your data within 30 days.

Security

Passwords are hashed with Argon2, sessions use signed cookies, and all traffic should be served over HTTPS in production. No method of transmission is 100% secure; we encourage you to use a unique password.
